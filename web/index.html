Fair Ticketing, Powered by Solana. Zero Scalping, 100% Transparent.

A ticket platform that ends the black market: fair face-value pricing, a hard price cap on resale, and automatic royalties for organizers.

1Fair primary sale

Tickets are sold at the organizer's face price, minted straight to your wallet.

2Price Cap on resale

Resale is capped at 120% of face value. The cap is enforced on chain, so scalping is not possible.

3Automatic 5% royalty

On every resale, 5% goes to the organizer and 95% to the seller, split in one transaction.
